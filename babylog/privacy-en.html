Oh My Baby Privacy Policy

Information stored on your device

The app keeps your baby's name, birthday, due date and profile photo, feed, diaper, sleep, bath, solids and pumping records, temperature, hospital and vaccine visits, stool color and texture, milestones, solid food ingredients, height, weight, head circumference, and app settings on your phone. If you turn on neither caregiver sharing nor cloud backup (called "Linked account" in the app), WaveWorks does not receive this information on a server, cannot see it, and does not use it for ads. Deleting the app deletes the information on the device. A backup file holds the records and your baby's profile photo. You choose where the app saves backup and CSV files and who gets a copy. The app does not upload these files to a server.

If you turn on caregiver sharing

Caregiver sharing stays off until you turn it on in Settings. Before it starts, the app lists on a consent screen the information it sends to the server, and sharing starts after you tap "Agree and start". Once sharing is on, the caregivers in the same family see and edit the information below.

Retention and deletion of shared information

  • Shared information stays on the server while at least one caregiver remains in the family.
  • When a caregiver taps "Stop sharing", the app removes that phone from the family and deletes its anonymous account. Records that caregiver made stay in the family, and the phone keeps a copy of the records up to that moment.
  • If the server refuses to delete the account (for example, an old sign-in), the app signs out instead. The cleanup job deletes that account under the 30-day rule below, because it belongs to no family.
  • Only the caregiver who manages the family (the one who created it, or the next earliest member if that caregiver left) can remove another phone. A removed phone stops sharing and deletes its anonymous account the next time it connects. The copy on the phone stays.
  • When the last caregiver taps "Stop sharing", the app deletes the baby and records on the server and closes the family. Nobody can open a closed family, and a cleanup job that runs once a day deletes everything left in families closed for more than a day.
  • A record you delete in the app stays in "Recently deleted" for 30 days. After that, or when you choose "Delete forever", the app erases the server copy's content. A phone in the family runs the 30-day step when the app opens, so if no phone opens the app, the deleted record's content stays on the server longer than 30 days. A mark holding the record ID, the deletion time and the deleting phone's anonymous ID stays for 365 days so that another phone cannot bring the record back, and then the cleanup job deletes it.
  • An invite code stops working 24 hours after it is created, and Firestore's automatic deletion removes expired codes.
  • The cleanup job deletes an anonymous account that belongs to no family 30 days after its last use.
  • If no phone in a family uses sharing for a year, the cleanup job closes the family and, the next day, deletes its baby, records, deletion marks, invite codes and family information. The members' anonymous accounts follow under the 30-day rule. If a member signed in with Apple or Google (cloud backup), the cleanup job leaves the family open and only updates its last-used time. On a day the cleanup job cannot check the members' accounts, it skips the family and checks again the next day.
  • WaveWorks keeps family purchase records (the purchase number hash, the family ID, check and refund times) so that a refund reaches the right family and one purchase applies to one family. They remain after the cleanup job deletes the family information.
  • If you deleted the app before stopping sharing, or all caregivers lost their phones, you can ask for deletion by email. Without a request, the one-year rule above applies.

If you turn on cloud backup

The app screens call cloud backup "Linked account". Cloud backup stays off until you tap "Link account" under Menu > Backup and sign in with Apple or Google. Before you sign in, a consent screen lists what goes to the server (with Google sign-in, also your name and profile photo link), what stays on the phone, where the data is stored and how to turn backup off. When you restore records with "Start from a backup" on the first screen, the same consent screen appears before you sign in. If the account you signed in with has no backup and you start fresh with it, backup turns on only after you tap "Agree and start" on the consent screen. With backup on, you can sign in with the same account on a new phone and get your records back.

  • If you choose "Hide My Email" for Sign in with Apple, the account keeps the forwarding address that Apple creates.
  • From Sign in with Apple, the app keeps the email and does not send your name to the server. On iPhone, the Apple sign-in sheet asks whether to share your name as well as your email.
  • A user with backup alone also gets a one-caregiver family on the server. If you share with caregivers, that family copy is your backup, and the app does not send the same records twice.
  • The app does not send baby photos, app settings or ad consent to the server.
  • Turning backup on or off and restoring from it each send an event to Firebase Analytics, and a user property records whether backup is on. Each event carries the sign-in type (Apple or Google) and nothing else. If a backup step fails, the app sends Crashlytics the step name and error code. None of these carry record content, email or user ID.

  • The app encrypts data in transit (TLS), and Google encrypts stored data by default. The app does not use end-to-end encryption.
  • During caregiver sharing or cloud backup, the app uses Firebase App Check to confirm that requests come from the genuine app from the store on an unmodified device. Apple App Attest judges this on iPhone and Google Play Integrity on Android, and the app sends the resulting check token with its requests. Three server functions (family purchase check, account deletion, caregiver seat cleanup) refuse requests without a valid token. The check does not use record content, email or user ID.

Retention and deletion of cloud backup information

  • WaveWorks keeps the backup copy and the sign-in account until you turn backup off or delete the account.
  • "Unlink and delete" under Menu > Backup and "Delete account" at the bottom of Settings do the same thing. After you sign in with Apple or Google again to confirm it is you, the app deletes the backup copy, the account entry and the sign-in account on the spot. Records on your phone stay.
  • Settings shows "Delete account" even after you sign out or reinstall the app. Sign in with the account you want to delete, and the app shows its email address and asks once more. "Delete" removes that account's server backup and sign-in account and signs out. "Cancel" only signs out. The app keeps the records on this phone and does not upload them. The app hides this row while you use caregiver sharing without signing in and while you wait to join a family. With cloud backup and caregiver sharing together, the row stays visible.
  • If you delete in the iPhone app after signing in with Apple, the app also revokes the Apple sign-in link. Deleting in the Android app or by email does not revoke it, and you can remove the app yourself from the "Sign in with Apple" list in iPhone Settings or on the Apple Account website. If revoking fails, the app still goes on to delete the account. In the rare case that the App Check blocks the server deletion, the app has already revoked the Apple link, and the account stays in "Deleting your backup". When you delete again, Apple asks for your sign-in consent once more.
  • If the app cannot pass the App Check, it cannot delete the account in the app and points you to the account deletion section of this privacy policy. You can then ask by email as that section and the account deletion page describe. On a device with backup on, you can also choose "Turn off backup on this phone". That button signs out without contacting the server. After that, it clears only this device's backup and sharing settings and its upload list. The records on the device and the server backup and account stay, so you delete the server side by email.
  • If you share with other caregivers, only your account leaves the family, and the records you left there stay for the other caregivers.
  • If the connection drops before deletion finishes, the app finishes it the next time you open it and uploads nothing in the meantime.
  • "Sign out" stops backup on this device. The server copy and the sign-in account stay.
  • If you deleted the app, reinstall it, go in without signing in and use "Delete account" in Settings, or ask by email as described on the account deletion page (<URL>). We delete within 30 days of an email request and reply with the result.
  • WaveWorks keeps a recovery copy of the server database (Firestore point-in-time recovery) in case of an operating error. A deleted backup stays in that copy for 7 days and then disappears.
  • WaveWorks does not delete unused backups on its own. The one-year rule above skips families with a member signed in with Apple or Google, so the backup copy and the sign-in account stay until you delete them, even if no device with backup on opens the app for a long time.
  • Family purchase records stay after you delete the account, for the same reason as with caregiver sharing.

Processors

WaveWorks uses Google LLC (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions) to store and process caregiver sharing and cloud backup information.

International transfers

The companies behind these services operate in the United States. While you use the app, it sends the information in the table above over the network to Google LLC (AdMob, Firebase) and RevenueCat, Inc. in the United States. Cloud Firestore keeps the caregiver sharing records in the Seoul region. Each company keeps the information for the period set in its own policy (links below). If you do not want this transfer, delete the app. Deleting it stops the transfer. If you turned on caregiver sharing or cloud backup, the server information stays after you delete the app, so tap "Stop sharing" or "Delete account" first, or ask for deletion by email.

Ads

The app does not request ads based on your interests (personalized ads) and does not ask for tracking permission on iOS. Non-personalized ads still use the advertising ID to limit how often you see the same ad and to count ad views. In regions that require consent, such as the European Economic Area, the app shows Google's consent screen on first launch. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in your device settings. The app does not use baby records, caregiver sharing information, cloud backup information or your sign-in email for ads.

Children

Parents and caregivers use this app. It is not directed to children and does not collect information from children. Caregivers enter the baby's information themselves.

Retention and deletion

Records on your device stay until you delete them or delete the app. If you never turn on caregiver sharing or cloud backup, you have no account and WaveWorks holds no server records about you. WaveWorks deletes caregiver sharing information as described in "Retention and deletion of shared information" and cloud backup information as described in "Retention and deletion of cloud backup information". Third-party services keep the information they collect under their own policies.
