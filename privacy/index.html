Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026 · 시행일: 2026년 10월 9일

English

This policy explains how WaveWorks ("we") handles information in our mobile apps, including Bus Loop Escape (Korean title: 스쿨버스 지옥탈출). We do not operate our own servers, user accounts, or cloud saves, and we do not ask for your name, email address, or other contact details inside the apps.

1. Information stored on your device

Game progress, coins, items, and settings are saved only on your device. We cannot access this data. Deleting the app or clearing its data removes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Our apps show ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data. In regions where consent is required (such as the EEA and the UK), the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later from the app's Settings screen. You can also reset or opt out of the advertising ID in your device settings.

See Google's policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Crash reporting, analytics, and remote settings (Firebase)

Our apps use three Firebase services provided by Google LLC. Google receives the data listed below and processes it on its servers under its own policies.

  • Firebase Crashlytics receives error details when the app crashes or hits an error it cannot recover from: the error message and, for native crashes, the stack trace (the message can contain app-internal values), app version, device model, OS version, and a Crashlytics installation ID. We use it to improve stability.
  • Firebase Analytics receives an app instance ID (an identifier for your installation; on iOS, also the identifier for vendor) and usage events: level start, clear, and fail, with the level number, attempt count, time spent, number of taps, bump count, whether you continued after failing, number of boosters used, and number of open slots; whether you watched a rewarded ad and whether it paid out; when an interstitial ad was due; the product ID of a completed purchase; and screen views (main and play). Firebase also records standard events it generates itself, such as first open and session start. It also receives your device model, OS, language, and approximate region based on your IP address. We use it to improve difficulty, ads, and the shop.
  • Firebase Remote Config sends the app instance ID along with basic app and device information (app version, OS, language) to Google's servers so the app can download settings such as ad frequency. The app adds no other information that identifies you.

Firebase does not receive your name, email address, phone number, or precise location, and the apps have no accounts. Firebase Analytics is set not to collect the advertising ID on Android, and the iOS app does not ask for tracking permission, so the iOS advertising ID is not available to it. Google AdMob uses the advertising ID as described in section 2. The apps have no setting that turns off Firebase collection alone. Uninstalling the app stops it. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

4. In-app purchases (Google Play, App Store, RevenueCat)

Payments are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never receive your card or payment details. We use RevenueCat to check which purchases you own and to restore them. RevenueCat processes an anonymous app user ID, purchase and transaction records, and basic device information. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

5. How long information is kept

On-device data is kept until you delete the app. Data processed by Google and RevenueCat is kept according to their own policies.

6. Children

Our apps are intended for users aged 13 and older and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Your rights

You can stop all data collection by uninstalling the app. To ask about your information or make a request, contact us at the email below.

8. Changes

If we add new services, we will update this page and the effective date before the change takes effect.

3. 오류 수집·이용 통계·원격 설정 (Firebase)

회사의 앱은 Google LLC가 제공하는 Firebase 서비스 세 가지를 사용합니다. 아래 정보는 Google 서버로 전송되며 Google의 정책에 따라 처리됩니다.

  • Firebase Crashlytics: 앱이 비정상 종료하거나 복구할 수 없는 오류가 났을 때 오류 메시지와 앱이 비정상 종료한 경우의 스택 트레이스(오류 메시지에 앱 내부 값이 포함될 수 있음), 앱 버전, 기기 모델, OS 버전, Crashlytics 설치 ID를 수집합니다. 앱 안정성을 높이는 데 씁니다.
  • Firebase Analytics: 앱 인스턴스 ID(설치 단위 식별자, iOS는 벤더 식별자 포함)와 앱 사용 이벤트를 수집합니다. 이벤트는 레벨 시작·클리어·실패와 해당 레벨 번호·시도 횟수·소요 시간·탭 수·충돌 횟수·이어하기 여부·부스터 사용 수·열린 슬롯 수, 보상형 광고 시청 여부와 보상 지급 여부, 전면 광고 노출 차례, 구매에 성공한 상품 ID, 화면 전환(메인·플레이)입니다. Firebase가 자동으로 기록하는 첫 실행·세션 시작 같은 기본 이벤트도 포함됩니다. 기기 모델, OS, 언어, IP 주소 기반의 대략적 지역도 수집합니다. 난이도, 광고, 상점을 개선하는 데 씁니다.
  • Firebase Remote Config: 광고 빈도 같은 앱 설정 값을 받아 오기 위해 앱 인스턴스 ID와 앱·기기 기본 정보(앱 버전, OS, 언어)를 Google 서버로 보냅니다. 앱이 이용자를 식별하는 다른 정보를 더 보내지는 않습니다.

Firebase로 이름, 이메일, 전화번호, 정확한 위치는 수집하지 않으며, 앱에는 회원 계정이 없습니다. Firebase Analytics는 Android에서 광고 식별자를 수집하지 않도록 설정했고, iOS 앱은 추적 허용을 요청하지 않아 iOS 광고 식별자를 받을 수 없습니다. AdMob은 2번 항목에 적은 대로 광고 식별자를 사용합니다. 앱에는 Firebase 수집만 따로 끄는 설정이 없고, 앱을 삭제하면 수집이 멈춥니다. 자세한 내용은 Firebase 개인정보 보호 및 보안을 참고하세요.
