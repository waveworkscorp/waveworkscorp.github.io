Yokai Hunters Privacy Policy

Developer: WaveWorks (Representative: Paul Hong) · Contact: hongp0508@gmail.com · Effective date: 2026-10-26

1. Summary

Yokai Hunters has no sign-up, no accounts, and no server run by the developer. Your game progress and settings stay on your device. Some information is sent to the external services listed below for ads, usage statistics, error reports, and purchase checks.

2. Information stored on your device

The following information is stored on your device and is not sent to the developer's server.

  • Game progress: hero and summon shards and grades, gear, charm books, stage records, gold and other resources, mailbox status
  • Settings: music and sound effects, language, display options, battle speed, damage number display
  • The time you agreed to the Terms of Service and this Privacy Policy on first launch

Uninstalling the app deletes this information. Progress cannot be moved to another device.

4. Ad consent and withdrawal

First launch

On first launch you must agree to the Terms of Service and this Privacy Policy before you can start playing. The app stores the time of your agreement on your device.

Ad consent

In regions that require ad consent, Google's consent form (UMP) appears. If you do not allow personalized ads, you will see non-personalized ads. The advertising identifier is also used to count ad impressions and prevent invalid clicks.

To change your choice, press Consent settings in the settings popup.

The app sends the game events in section 2 to Firebase Analytics only when your consent allows ad requests. Error reports, screen names, and the basic data Firebase collects on its own (such as first open and app usage time) are sent regardless of consent.

You can also reset or delete the advertising identifier in your device settings to limit ad tracking.

On iOS, the app asks for tracking permission once, the first time you watch a rewarded ad. If you allow it, AdMob can use the iOS advertising identifier; if you decline, it cannot. The game works the same either way, and you can change the choice in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Information on your device is deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • External services keep information under their own policies. You can change ad personalization in your Google ad settings.
  • The developer does not store user information on a server, so there is no developer-held data to delete.
  • Purchase records are kept by the store and RevenueCat. See your store account or the RevenueCat privacy policy.

The app is intended for users aged 12 and older. It is not made for children, and it does not ask for sign-ups, names, or contact details.
