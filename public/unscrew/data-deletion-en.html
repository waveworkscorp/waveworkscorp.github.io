Delete your Screw Punch 3D data

Screw Punch 3D (developer: WaveWorks) has no user accounts. Your game progress is saved on your phone. If you use backup, Google Play Games or Apple Game Center keeps one more copy. WaveWorks does not keep user data on its own servers.

Progress on your phone

Level progress, coins, boosters, and settings live only on your phone. Uninstall the app, or clear its data in Android settings, and they are gone.

Cloud backup (Android)

Your backup is saved in your Google Play Games account. To delete it:

  1. Open your profile in the Google Play Games app or the Play Store, then open Settings.
  2. Open "Delete Play Games account & data".
  3. Under "Delete individual game data", find Screw Punch 3D and tap Delete.

This removes the backup, achievements, and leaderboard scores together. Menu names can differ a little between Play Games versions.

Cloud backup (iPhone and iPad)

Your backup is a Game Center saved game stored in your iCloud. In iPhone Settings, tap your name, then iCloud, then Manage Storage. If Screw Punch 3D is in the list, tap it and delete its data. Leaderboard scores and achievements stay in your Game Center account. You can change who sees your profile under Game Center in Settings.

Request by email

The records below cannot be deleted in the app. Email hongp0508@gmail.com with the subject "Screw Punch 3D data deletion".

  • Purchase records (payment service RevenueCat): the app records purchases under a random ID, with no name or email. To find your records we need an order number. Include the order number from your Google Play receipt (starts with GPA.) or the order ID from your Apple receipt. Without a request, we keep purchase records as long as needed to restore purchases and meet legal obligations.
  • Analytics and crash records (Google Firebase): these are stored only under a random ID made for each device, with no name, email, or order number, so we cannot find you from an email. Uninstalling the app stops new records. Google deletes analytics records after 2 months and crash records after 90 days. The installation identifier for remote settings stays until deletion is requested, then is deleted within 180 days.

Timing

  • Deleting on your phone, in Play Games, or in iCloud takes effect right away.
  • We handle email requests within 30 days of receiving them and reply to the address you wrote from.

What stays

  • Your purchase history in the app stores. Google Play and Apple keep it under their own policies.
  • The advertising ID used by our ad service (Google AdMob). You can reset it or turn it off in your phone settings.
