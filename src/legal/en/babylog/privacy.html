Oh My Baby Privacy Policy

1. Information stored on your device

The app keeps your baby's name, birthday, due date and profile photo, feed, diaper, sleep, bath, solids and pumping records, temperature, hospital and vaccine visits, stool color and texture, milestones, solid food ingredients, height, weight, head circumference, and app settings on your phone. If you turn on neither caregiver sharing nor cloud backup (called "Linked account" in the app), WaveWorks does not receive this information on a server, cannot see it, and does not use it for ads. Deleting the app deletes the information on the device. A backup file holds the records and your baby's profile photo. You choose where the app saves backup and CSV files and who gets a copy. The app does not upload these files to a server.

2. If you turn on caregiver sharing

Caregiver sharing stays off until you turn it on in Settings. Before it starts, a consent screen shows what information goes to the server and why, where it is stored, how long it is kept, your right to refuse and what changes if you refuse, and how to withdraw. Saving your baby's health-related records (collection) and viewing them with caregivers (sharing) are separate checkboxes, and none is pre-checked. The caregiver who creates the family also checks "I am this baby’s parent or legal guardian and I agree on the baby’s behalf". Sharing starts only after you check every box and tap "Agree and continue". If you do not agree, the features on your device keep working. The app keeps a record of what you agreed to, when, and which wording version, on the device and on the server (as that device's consent record inside the family). Once sharing is on, the caregivers in the same family see and edit the information below.

3. Retention and deletion of shared information

  • Shared information stays on the server while at least one caregiver remains in the family.
  • When a caregiver taps "Stop sharing", the app removes that phone from the family and deletes its anonymous account. Records that caregiver made stay in the family, and the phone keeps a copy of the records up to that moment.
  • If the server refuses to delete the account (for example, an old sign-in), the app signs out instead. The cleanup job deletes that account under the 30-day rule below, because it belongs to no family.
  • Only the caregiver who manages the family (the one who created it, or the next earliest member if that caregiver left) can remove another phone. A removed phone stops sharing and deletes its anonymous account the next time it connects. The copy on the phone stays.
  • When the last caregiver taps "Stop sharing", the app deletes the baby and records on the server and closes the family. Nobody can open a closed family, and a cleanup job that runs once a day deletes everything left in families closed for more than a day.
  • A record you delete in the app stays in "Recently deleted" for 30 days. After that, or when you choose "Delete forever", the app erases the server copy's content. A server cleanup job runs every day even if no phone opens the app. It erases the content of records deleted more than 30 days ago and deletes deleted baby entries whole. A mark holding the record ID, the deletion time and the deleting phone's anonymous ID stays for 365 days so that another phone cannot bring the record back, and then the cleanup job deletes it.
  • An invite code stops working 24 hours after it is created, and Firestore's automatic deletion removes expired codes.
  • The cleanup job deletes an anonymous account that belongs to no family 30 days after its last use.
  • If no phone in a family uses sharing for a year, the cleanup job closes the family and, the next day, deletes its baby, records, deletion marks, invite codes and family information. The members' anonymous accounts follow under the 30-day rule. If a member signed in with Apple or Google (cloud backup), the cleanup job leaves the family open and only updates its last-used time. On a day the cleanup job cannot check the members' accounts, it skips the family and checks again the next day.
  • WaveWorks keeps the family purchase record (the purchase number hash, the family ID, the check time) while the purchase is valid, so that one purchase applies to only one family. It also stays after the family information is deleted, so the purchase cannot be reused by another family. If the purchase is refunded or canceled, the family ID is erased and only a refund marker stays (the purchase number hash and the check and refund times). The marker keeps a refunded purchase from being applied again when a refund and a purchase check arrive out of order. The marker does not identify a family or a person.
  • If you deleted the app before stopping sharing, or all caregivers lost their phones, you can ask for deletion by email. Without a request, the one-year rule above applies.

4. If you turn on cloud backup

The app screens call cloud backup "Linked account". Cloud backup stays off until you tap "Sign in" under Settings > Backup and sign in with Apple or Google. Before you sign in, a consent screen lists what goes to the server (with Google sign-in, also your name and profile photo link), what stays on the phone, where the data is stored and how to turn backup off. When you restore records with "Sign in to start" on the first screen, the same consent screen appears before you sign in. If the account you signed in with has no backup and you start fresh with it, backup turns on only after you tap "Agree and continue" on the consent screen. With backup on, you can sign in with the same account on a new phone and get your records back.

5. Retention and deletion of cloud backup information

  • WaveWorks keeps the backup copy and the sign-in account until you turn backup off or delete the account.
  • "Unlink and delete" under Settings > Backup and "Delete account" at the bottom of Settings do the same thing. After you sign in with Apple or Google again to confirm it is you, the app deletes the backup copy, the account entry and the sign-in account on the spot. Records on your phone stay.
  • Settings shows "Delete account" even after you sign out or reinstall the app. Sign in with the account you want to delete, and the app shows its email address and asks once more. "Delete" removes that account's server backup and sign-in account and signs out. "Cancel" only signs out. The app keeps the records on this phone and does not upload them. The app hides this row while you use caregiver sharing without signing in and while you wait to join a family. With cloud backup and caregiver sharing together, the row stays visible.
  • If you delete in the iPhone app after signing in with Apple, the app also revokes the Apple sign-in link. Deleting in the Android app or by email does not revoke it, and you can remove the app yourself from the "Sign in with Apple" list in iPhone Settings or on the Apple Account website. If revoking fails, the app still goes on to delete the account. In the rare case that the App Check blocks the server deletion, the app has already revoked the Apple link, and the account stays in "Deleting your backup". When you delete again, Apple asks for your sign-in consent once more.
  • If you stop using Sign in with Apple for this app in iPhone Settings or on the Apple Account website, or delete your Apple Account, Apple notifies the WaveWorks server. When Apple reports that you stopped using it, the server signs that account out and keeps the backup copy. Sign in again with the same Apple Account and the backup carries on. When Apple reports a deleted Apple Account, the server deletes the backup copy, the account entry and the sign-in account, the same as "Delete account". If that account also has Google sign-in linked, the server signs it out and removes only the Apple link, and the backup carries on with Google sign-in. For a change to email forwarding, the server records only the notice type.
  • If the app cannot pass the App Check, it cannot delete the account in the app and points you to the account deletion section of this privacy policy. You can then ask by email as that section and the account deletion page describe. On a device with backup on, you can also choose "Turn off backup on this phone". That button signs out without contacting the server. After that, it clears only this device's backup and sharing settings and its upload list. The records on the device and the server backup and account stay, so you delete the server side by email.
  • If you share with other caregivers, only your account leaves the family, and the records you left there stay for the other caregivers.
  • If the connection drops before deletion finishes, the app finishes it the next time you open it and uploads nothing in the meantime.
  • "Sign out" stops backup on this device. The server copy and the sign-in account stay.
  • If you deleted the app, reinstall it, go in without signing in and use "Delete account" in Settings, or ask by email as described on the account deletion page (<URL>). We delete within 10 days of an email request and reply with the result.
  • WaveWorks keeps a recovery copy of the server database (Firestore point-in-time recovery) in case of an operating error. Deleted shared and backup information also stays in that copy for 7 days and then disappears. The copy never appears in the service and is used only to recover from an outage. If the database is rolled back to the copy, WaveWorks deletes again any information that had a deletion request in the meantime and logs that it did so.
  • WaveWorks does not delete unused backups on its own. The one-year rule above skips families with a member signed in with Apple or Google, so the backup copy and the sign-in account stay until you delete them, even if no device with backup on opens the app for a long time.
  • Family purchase records stay after you delete the account, for the same reason as with caregiver sharing.

9. Ads

The app does not request ads based on your interests (personalized ads) and does not ask for tracking permission on iOS. Non-personalized ads still use the advertising ID to limit how often you see the same ad and to count ad views. In regions that require consent, such as the European Economic Area, the app shows Google's consent screen on first launch. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in your device settings. The app does not use baby records, caregiver sharing information, cloud backup information or your sign-in email for ads.

10. Children

Parents and caregivers use this app. It is not directed to children and does not collect information directly from children. Caregivers, meaning parents or legal representatives, enter the baby's information themselves. Caregiver sharing and cloud backup send information about a baby, who is a child under 14, to the server. They turn on only after the caregiver who creates the family checks "I am this baby’s parent or legal guardian and I agree on the baby’s behalf" on the consent screen. The consent record keeps that check, the time and the wording version. The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If we learn that a child provided information directly, we delete it.

13. Your rights and contact

You and your baby's legal representative can ask to see, correct or delete the information on the server, to stop its processing, or to withdraw your consent. In the app you can do this right away with "Stop sharing", "Unlink and delete" or "Delete account". If you cannot use the app, ask at the email below.

  • Verifying who is asking: for a cloud backup account, write from the same email address as the account you signed in with. If you used caregiver sharing only, we find the family from the baby's name and birthday and the approximate date sharing started. If your representative is not a legal representative, please send a power of attorney with the request.
  • We handle the request within 10 days of receiving it and reply with the result. If we refuse or postpone a request, we also tell you the reason and how to object within 10 days.
  • If you disagree with the result, you can ask for help from the Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee (www.kopico.go.kr, 1833-6972) or the Personal Information Infringement Report Center (privacy.kisa.or.kr, dial 118 with no area code).
