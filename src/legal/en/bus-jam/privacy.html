Bus Loop Escape Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

WaveWorks ("we") sets out this privacy policy to protect users' personal information under the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea. It applies to our mobile game Bus Loop Escape (Korean title: 스쿨버스 지옥탈출). We do not run our own servers, user accounts, or cloud saves, and we do not ask for your name, email address, or other contact details inside the apps.

1. Information stored on your device

Game progress, coins, items, and settings are saved only on your device. We cannot access this data. Deleting the app or clearing its data removes it.

3. Crash reports, analytics, and remote settings (Firebase)

The game uses three Firebase services provided by Google LLC. The information below is sent to Google's servers and processed under Google's policies.

  • Firebase Crashlytics: when the app crashes or hits an error it cannot recover from, it collects the error message and, for crashes, the stack trace (the message can contain app-internal values), app version, device model, OS version, and Crashlytics installation ID. We use it to improve stability.
  • Firebase Analytics: collects an app instance ID (an identifier for your installation; on iOS, also the identifier for vendor) and app usage events: level start, clear, and fail, with the level number, attempt count, time spent, number of taps, bump count, whether you continued, number of boosters used, and number of open slots; whether you watched a rewarded ad and whether the reward was given; when an interstitial ad was due; the product ID of a completed purchase; and screen views (main and play). Standard events Firebase records itself, such as first open and session start, are included. It also collects device model, OS, language, and approximate region based on your IP address. We use it to improve difficulty, ads, and the shop.
  • Firebase Remote Config: sends the app instance ID and basic app and device information (app version, OS, language) to Google's servers to download app settings such as ad frequency. The app sends no other information that identifies you.

Firebase does not collect your name, email address, phone number, or precise location, and the game has no accounts. Firebase Analytics is set not to collect the advertising identifier on Android. The iOS app does not ask for tracking permission, so it cannot receive the iOS advertising identifier. AdMob uses the advertising identifier as described in section 2. The game has no setting that turns off Firebase collection alone; uninstalling the app stops it. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

4. Retention and deletion

Information stored on your device is kept until you delete the app and is destroyed with it. We keep no personal information of our own.

5. Children

The game is intended for users aged 13 and older and are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
