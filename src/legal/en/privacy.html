Website analytics

This website (waveworkscorp.github.io) uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, provided by Cloudflare, Inc., to understand how many people visit and which pages they read. It does not use cookies or browser storage and does not store information that identifies individual visitors.

Information processed: the page address you visit, the previous page address (referrer), your country, browser, operating system and device type, and page load performance.

Cloudflare processes this information and we only see aggregated statistics. Cloudflare keeps the statistics available for up to six months. Content blockers prevent this collection.

Cloudflare, Inc. is a U.S. company, and this information may be processed outside the Republic of Korea, including in the United States and other regions where Cloudflare operates servers.

These statistics are not linked to any information inside our apps or games.
