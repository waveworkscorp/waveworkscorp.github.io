Bus Loop Escape, the first WaveWorks game, went live on Google Play on October 19. We founded the studio in October 2025, so it's our first release, a year in.

You clear a packed parking lot one bus at a time and send passengers off in the bus that matches their color. With over 300 stages and no timer, you can take it slow with one finger.

## How to play

![Tapping out the buses that are free to move](/works/bus-jam/shots/en-1.jpg)

1. Tap a bus that has a clear way out of the lot.
2. Passengers of the same color climb aboard.
3. Send every passenger off to clear the stage.

That's all the rules there are. Pick the wrong bus first, though, and the parking spots fill up fast. Working out which bus should leave next is where the fun is.

## What changes as you go

Later stages add more bus colors and trickier lots. The background changes every 10 stages, starting in a park and moving through a plaza, a beach and a playground to a snow town.

![A hidden bus showing its color once the bus in front leaves](/works/bus-jam/shots/en-2.jpg)

From stage 22, special buses join in. A hidden bus keeps its color secret until the bus in front of it moves out. Container buses start showing up at the same point.

| What changes | When |
|---|---|
| More bus colors, trickier lots | As you progress |
| The background moves through park, plaza, beach, playground and snow town | Every 10 stages |
| Hidden buses and container buses show up | From stage 22 |

## Boosters when you're stuck, coins when you win

![Using a booster to pick a bus out of the lot](/works/bus-jam/shots/en-3.jpg)

When you can't see a way out, reach for one of four boosters: VIP, Sort, Shuffle or Open a Spot. With a booster, you can use one to pick a stuck bus out of the lot. Every stage you clear earns you coins.

## Before you download

- Runs on Android 7.0 and up. The iOS version is in progress.
- Rated Everyone on Google Play.
- Available in Korean, English, Japanese, and Simplified and Traditional Chinese.
- Has rewarded ads, plus interstitials from stage 11. You can buy ad removal, coins and bundles in the app.
- Ads and purchases need an internet connection.

[Get it on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.waveworks.busloopescape)
