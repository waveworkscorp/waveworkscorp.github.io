The parking lot is jammed. Buses of every color are tangled together, and each passenger will only ride a bus of their own color. Your job is to pick which bus goes first.

Bus Loop Escape, the first game from WaveWorks, came out on Google Play on October 19. [Get it here.](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.waveworks.busloopescape)

## One tap, one bus

![Tapping out the buses that are free to move](/works/bus-jam/shots/en-1.jpg)

Tap a bus with a clear path and it drives out of the lot. Passengers of the same color hop on, and once everyone has left, the level is done.

Sounds easy. Pick the wrong order, though, and the parking spots fill up fast. When the last free spots start to vanish, you slow down and start thinking. That's the best part of the game.

## The lot keeps getting messier

There are more than 300 levels. Later ones bring more bus colors and trickier lots. The scenery changes every 10 levels, from the park to the plaza, the beach, the playground and finally a snowy town.

![A hidden bus showing its color after the bus in front leaves](/works/bus-jam/shots/en-2.jpg)

From level 22, some buses hide their color. You only see it after the bus in front moves, so you have to plan one step further. Container buses show up from that point too.

When you're truly stuck, there are boosters: VIP, Sort, Shuffle and Open Parking Spot. Clear a level and you earn coins.

## No clock

There's no timer. Finish fast or stare at one bus for a while; either is fine. Of everything in the game, this is what our founder most wanted players to know. If you like tidy-up puzzles, you can solve these at your own pace.

A few things to check before you download:

- Runs on Android 7.0 and up. An iOS version is on the way.
- Rated Everyone on Google Play.
- Available in Korean, English, Japanese, and Simplified and Traditional Chinese.
- Has rewarded ads, plus interstitial ads from level 11. You can buy ad removal, coins and bundles in the app.
- Ads and purchases need an internet connection.
