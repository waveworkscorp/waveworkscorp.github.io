Bus Loop Escape, the first game from WaveWorks, came out on Google Play on October 19. [Get it on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.waveworks.busloopescape)

It's a puzzle about pulling tangled, color-coded buses out one at a time and sending off passengers of the same color. Tap a bus with a clear path and it drives out of the lot; once every passenger has left, the level is done. Pick the wrong order and the free parking spots fill up fast. With two or three spots left, you stop and work out which bus has to go first. Our founder calls that the most fun moment in the game.

![Tapping out the buses that are free to move](/works/bus-jam/shots/en-1.jpg)

We left out a timer on purpose, and it's the first thing we wanted to say about the game. You can stare at one bus for as long as you like, or play a single level and put the phone down. It's built for people who like tidy-up puzzles and want to solve them at their own pace.

There are more than 300 levels. From level 22, buses that hide their color until the one in front moves, and container buses, start mixing in. The scenery changes every 10 levels: park, plaza, beach, playground, snowy town. When you're stuck there are four boosters (VIP, Sort, Shuffle, Open Parking Spot), and clearing a level earns coins.

A few things to know before you download. It runs on Android 7.0 and up and is available in Korean, English, Japanese, and Simplified and Traditional Chinese. It has rewarded ads, plus interstitial ads from level 11, and sells ad removal, coins and bundles in the app. Ads and purchases need an internet connection.

We're working on the iOS version and will post here when it's out. If a level felt too easy or too hard, just send us its number through the [contact page](/en/contact/). That alone helps a lot.
